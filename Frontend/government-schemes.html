PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi

₹6,000 / Year Direct Cash Benefit

Direct income support transferred electronically into Aadhaar-linked bank accounts in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months to assist with farm input expenses.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

Small and marginal landholder farmer families with cultivable land up to 2 hectares in their name. Must have an active Aadhaar card and seeded bank account.

Required Documents & Process:

  • Aadhaar Card with mobile number linked for OTP verification
  • Landholding document / Jamabandi / Khatauni certificate
  • Bank passbook showing IFSC code and active account status
  • Online e-KYC authentication via biometric or OTP

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Credit up to ₹3,00,000 @ 4% Interest

Institutional credit facility enabling farmers to purchase seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, machinery fuel, and manage post-harvest cash flow without paying high private moneylender rates.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

All farmers including individual owner-cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, and members of Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) or Self-Help Groups (SHGs).

Key Loan Terms & Highlights:

  • Collateral-free loan limit up to ₹1,60,000
  • Subsidized interest rate: 7% base interest with 3% prompt repayment incentive = 4% net interest
  • Built-in crop insurance coverage under PM Fasal Bima Yojana
  • Simple one-page application process via local commercial or cooperative banks
🌐 View KCC Scheme Guidelines on MyScheme.gov.in →
🤝

FPO Formation & Promotion

Support up to ₹18 Lakh per FPO

National flagship mission to form 10,000 new Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) to pool smallholder harvests, achieve bulk scale economies, and sell directly to commercial supermarkets.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

Minimum 300 member farmers in plains or 100 farmers in hilly and North-Eastern regions registered under Companies Act or Cooperative Societies Act.

Financial & Incubation Assistance:

  • Direct management grant of up to ₹18 Lakhs over 3 years per FPO
  • Equity grant matching of up to ₹2,000 per farmer member (Max ₹15 Lakhs per FPO)
  • Credit Guarantee Facility cover up to ₹2 Crores with NABARD / NCDC
  • Dedicated handholding from Cluster-Based Business Organizations (CBBOs)
